# Validity Boundary Contract

Every material Delivery exposes or makes recoverable the conditions under which its dispositions are valid. The boundary records:

- explicit propositions;
- reviewed and unreviewed scope;
- the evidence snapshot and material evidence limitations;
- the executor profile;
- review and mutation authority;
- execution or resource limits; and
- the temporal reference.

A boundary qualifies a disposition; it does not weaken its evidence requirement. Omitted or inaccessible material evidence remains explicit and normally requires `MAYBE`. A changed snapshot requires revalidation of affected propositions.

The machine-readable form is [`validity-boundary.schema.json`](../schemas/validity-boundary.schema.json). Version 3 run receipts require it. Earlier receipts remain valid historical records under their original schema version.
