# Adoption Loop

**Purpose:** Determine whether intended users or stakeholders can understand, acquire, configure, operate, integrate, and adopt the project successfully.

- **Scope:** onboarding, documentation, installation or access, configuration, examples, discoverability, accessibility, migration, upgrades, defaults, messages, deployment ergonomics, publication onboarding, project initialization and removal, capability claims, and expectation versus reality.
- **Inputs and evidence:** audience definitions, user journeys, instructions, interfaces, examples, support records, observed attempts, and user-visible behavior.
- **Owns:** findings whose proposition concerns successful use or adoption.
- **Observes:** implementation and maintenance evidence that affects users.
- **May recommend:** clearer paths, safer defaults, documentation, examples, interface changes, or user validation.
- **May not decide:** technical correctness, architecture, or business priority solely from usability evidence.
- **Must hand off:** behavioral defects to Implementation; structural causes to Architecture; stale material to Maintenance.
- **Finding types:** access barrier, comprehension gap, misleading instruction, unusable default, inaccessible interaction, migration risk, unverifiable installation, unsafe initialization guidance, unclear removal, or unsupported public capability claim.
- **Output:** canonical findings, journey evidence, handoffs, and one loop result.
- **Termination:** selected user journeys were attempted or their evidence limits recorded.
- **Rerun:** user-facing behavior, audience, instructions, or validation evidence changes.
- **Failure modes:** passing technically correct but unusable work, inventing users, judging prose without task context, or requiring conventional software distribution.
